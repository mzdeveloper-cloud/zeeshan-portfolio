STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Use the Quiz

Follow these simple steps to complete your assessment.

01

Enter Employee Code

Enter your assigned employee code on the login page.

02

Check Your Information

Confirm that the employee information shown on screen belongs to you.

03

Select Your Language

Choose English or Urdu according to your preferred language.

04

Answer the Questions

Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

05

Complete Within the Time

The assessment has a 25-minute time limit.

06

Submit Your Assessment

Review your answers and submit the assessment when you are ready.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should complete this assessment?

Employees who have been assigned an assessment should complete the quiz using their employee code.

How many questions are there?

The assessment contains 25 questions.

How much time is available?

The assessment is designed to be completed within 25 minutes.

Can I take the assessment in Urdu?

Yes. The assessment provides English and Urdu language options.

What happens after I submit?

Your assessment result is processed by the assessment system. Successful completion may provide access to a certificate.
